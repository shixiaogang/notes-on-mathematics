% Truncation at a fixed level c > 0 splits each term X_k into a large part and a truncated part
% Y_k = X_k 1{|X_k| <= c}; Y_k is further split into its mean E[Y_k] and the centred fluctuation Y_k - E[Y_k].
% Each piece is controlled by one of the three series of the three-series theorem.
