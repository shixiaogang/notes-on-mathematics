% Original analytic teaching diagram, 2026-11-12.
% Reduction of correlated Gaussian noise to parallel channels: the sender maps xtilde to x = U xtilde, the channel
% adds Z ~ N(0, Sigma) with Sigma = U Lambda U^T, the receiver forms ytilde = U^T y = xtilde + U^T Z, where
% U^T Z ~ N(0, Lambda) has independent components. Orthogonality keeps |x|_2 = |xtilde|_2, so budgets agree.
