% Original analytic teaching diagram, 2026-11-12.
% HSIC as a distance between two kernel means.  Left: product space with the joint law (mass on a curve, blue)
% and the product of the marginals (spread over the rectangle, light blue).  Right: the RKHS of the product kernel;
% the two kernel means are two points whose squared distance is HSIC.  A poorer kernel only sees a projection
% onto a smaller subspace (dashed line), where the two images may coincide.
