% Original analytic teaching diagram, 2026-11-12.
% Pushforward family: fixed base P0 (standard normal density, left), maps T_theta, T_theta' (middle),
% outputs P_theta (blue, right top) and P_theta' (yellow, right bottom).
% Illustration with T_theta(z)=exp(0.5 z) (log-normal output) and T_theta'(z)=1+0.5 z.
% Band B=[1.2,1.6] on the output axis; its preimage under T_theta is [2 ln 1.2, 2 ln 1.6]=[0.365,0.940].
% Densities: left phi(z) over z in [-3,3], scale x 5 mm/unit, y 40 mm/unit.
% Right top: log-normal(0,0.5^2) density over x in [0,3.2], x 10 mm/unit, y 12 mm/unit.
